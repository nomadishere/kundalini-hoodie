Kundalini without the cult.

Kundalini is one of the most beautiful ideas in yoga, and one of the most abused. Here's what the old teachings actually say, what went wrong in the West, and how to keep the practice and leave the cult.

What the old texts describe

In tantric and hatha yoga texts, kundalini is pictured as a coiled power resting at the base of the spine. Through breath, locks and focus, it rises through the central channel, passing the energy centers we now call chakras, until it reaches the crown. The 15th-century Hatha Yoga Pradipika describes the breath work and locks. A 16th-century text on the six chakras became widely known in English through Sir John Woodroffe's 1919 book The Serpent Power.

Notice the pattern: breath, locks, the spine, the crown. It's the same circuit Taoists describe and the same path the glow hood lights.

What went wrong

"Kundalini Yoga" as a brand came to the West through Yogi Bhajan, starting in 1969. In 2020, after former followers came forward, an independent investigation by An Olive Branch concluded it was "more likely than not" that he engaged in several kinds of sexual misconduct and abused his power as a spiritual leader. (3HO overview, Yoga Journal)

The report closed with a question his own community now asks: can people follow the teachings without following the teacher?

Keep the practice, drop the cult

  • No one owns your energy. The breath, the locks and the spine are yours. You don't need a guru's permission or a uniform.
  • Go to the roots. Teachers like Dhirendra Brahmachari wrote the practices down plainly, for everyone.
  • Red flags: secrecy, a teacher who claims special powers over your body, pressure to give money or sex, and any rule that you can't question.

Practice safely

Kundalini work can stir up strong feelings and sensations. Go slowly, stay grounded, rest your attention in your belly and feet if you feel overwhelmed, and pause the practice if it destabilizes your sleep or mood. If you have a history of psychosis, seizures or heart problems, talk to a doctor first.
